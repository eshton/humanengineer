I am still reading a lot of posts on LinkedIn that pose questions such as:

a) Would you switch to agents writing code?
b) Can agents write good code?
c) Is it economical to have agents write code?

My dudes, I have bad news for you: you are stuck on the wrong questions.

The reality is this: everyone uses agents now because everyone sees their
superiority. There is no point in arguing about IF agents will take over.
They already did. You just haven't checked your mailbox yet for the invite.

So the real question is this: do you go agentic, or do you get left behind?

There is no other option for businesses. If you fail to act now, someone
will vibe-code your product tomorrow, and soon you'll be out of business.
