A big part of successful communication at work is how you handle
disagreements. Most people know how to do it… ineffectively. 😅

First, you listen, then you start your response by acknowledging the other
party, and then you say… BUT.

"Yes, X can work, but I think Y is better."

And instantly, the dynamic is changed forever. 💥 This is because deep down,
everyone knows that everything you say BEFORE the BUT is bullshit. That
simple little word instantly changes how the other person will hear your
message: they won't. They instantly know that you are invalidating them
while seemingly acknowledging their point of view.

The person is instantly on the defensive. 🛡️ They stop listening and start
preparing to defend their point of view. And they cannot wait for their turn
to go back and argue more for their original point. This is the perfect
recipe for never-ending arguments and people ghosting each other. 👻 Not the
outcome you hoped for when trying to get people to do things.

So, here is the solution: replace the BUT with an AND. 💡

"Yes, X can work, and I think Y is even better."

Instantly feels different. Saying AND means that you extend what was said
before you. You add to the conversation; you don't contradict or dismiss it.
🤝 The person feels you are on their side, and together you are even better.
They will not feel the need to defend themselves, since AND means you are
both right, and together you can work out what is best.

Saying BUT means you may just want to get your way; saying AND invites
collaboration. 🤝 Saying BUT shows that you put yourself above the other
person; saying AND puts you next to them. Saying BUT uncovers your
negativity, contempt, or criticism; saying AND prompts a connection on
positive grounds. 🌱

Try it yourself. Replace the BUT with AND, and see the difference in the
reaction. Then share your experience. 👇 Funnily enough, this is a lot harder
than you might think. 😄
