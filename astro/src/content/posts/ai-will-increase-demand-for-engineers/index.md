AI will increase demand for software and engineers rather than replace them.
The early signs are starting to show. 🚀

Both Microsoft and Amazon recently announced a combined $3.5 billion
investment in new organisations whose sole purpose will be to work with
customers and integrate AI technology into their businesses. 🤖

Every single engagement that these engineers work on will then be an
opportunity for customers to build internal engineering teams and replace
expensive external providers to maintain and replicate the know-how in new
initiatives. 👩‍💻👨‍💻

Eventually, demand will grow as companies see the proven value in their
businesses. This is demonstrated in a recent analysis from Ramp that covers
21,000 US firms: "Firms that adopt AI grow headcount by 10.2% over the two
years following adoption."

The future is bright for software and engineers. ☀️
