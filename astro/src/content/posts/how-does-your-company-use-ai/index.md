A friend of mine who is a big AI enthusiast told me that at his current company, AI is still not used effectively. They still review every line of code, have no CI/CD, and manually execute bi-monthly releases.

I was baffled. It seems like the AI age is reaching companies more slowly than I imagined.

So I asked on LinkedIn: **what is your company's AI engineering workflow?** 📊

| Workflow | Votes |
| --- | --- |
| Complete agentic engineering | 14% |
| AI generated, manual review | 71% |
| AI assists with manual code | 14% |
| We only chat with AI for info | 0% |

Small sample (7 votes), but the picture is clear: most teams let AI write the code and then still review it line by line. What's yours like? 👇
