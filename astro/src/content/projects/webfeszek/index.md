*Webfészek* — Hungarian for "web nest" — is a love project. I've loved
websites for as long as I can remember, since I was a kid, and this is me
turning that into an offer for the people I keep running into: contractors
and small businesses.

Most of them have no online presence at all. The ones that do are often
paying a lot for a 2MB static site that then never gets updated — a page
that went stale the day it launched. I build them a fast, SEO-strong static
site with minimal integration, host it, and keep it current, all for one
flat monthly fee.

The plan is to integrate the service with AI agents, so my customers can
make changes themselves, automatically — no email, no waiting, no system to
learn.
