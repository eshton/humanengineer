*Webfészek* — Hungarian for "web nest" — is my passion project. I've loved
websites since I was 8, and this is an attempt to make one problem go away
for small businesses: the website itself. You run the business; we run the
site.

The pitch is a flat monthly fee (from €20 + VAT) that covers everything —
the build, fast and secure hosting on a global CDN, built-in SEO, Google
reviews on the page, and **unlimited content changes**. New price, fresh
photo, seasonal offer: you email it, we publish it, usually the same working
day. No system to learn, no hourly invoices, no plugin renewals, no setup
fee. Switching — the most expensive part of most offers — costs nothing:
migration, domain transfer, and full setup are all free, and the domain and
content stay yours throughout.

## Why it can be this cheap

Agencies bill hours for work a machine now does in seconds — coding pages,
resizing images, SEO, performance tuning, accessibility, sitemaps. Webfészek
hands that mechanical grind to state-of-the-art AI, so the price is a
fraction of the old way. What never gets automated is any decision about the
customer's business: a human reviews and approves every change before it
goes live. AI never has the last word on how a business appears.

## Built so AI can find you too

Search isn't just Google any more — people ask ChatGPT, Perplexity, and
other assistants "who's a good tradesperson near me?", and most
small-business sites are invisible to them. Two AI features are live in free
beta for hosting customers:

- **AI-agent optimisation** — services, prices, and facts published in a
  machine-readable way so assistants find and quote the business accurately.
- **An on-site AI agent** — an assistant that answers questions, qualifies
  the job, and books a time straight from the browser.

The Webfészek site runs this way itself: you can open it in your own AI
assistant and have it read the whole offer, answer anything, or start an
enquiry.
